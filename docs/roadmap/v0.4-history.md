## Goal

Let a deployment decide per application whether a judge is needed. Add a deterministic
grounding check that catches invented values, which no tested judge caught. Let the
service prepare PDFs itself (a text layer, rendered pages) so cheap models that do not read
PDFs natively can be used without the caller sending pages. The TravelInbox contract stays
exact under the default policy (`always`). The other policies are opt-in supersets with a
new status `extracted` and a `verification` field.

Motivation: the owner's runs on ten real documents (2026-10-17). Cheap models reached 8 of
10 fully correct at about 0.6 cents per document. Every judge accepted an event invoice with
invented dates, venue, organiser and travellers. The judge is the most expensive step,
emails were right on the first attempt, and PDFs had to be rasterized by hand for models
without native PDF input. Decisions: [ADR 0009](../architecture/decisions/0009-judge-policy-and-grounding.md)
(judge policy and grounding) and [ADR 0010](../architecture/decisions/0010-pdf-preprocessing-in-service.md)
(PDF preprocessing in the service, amends ADR 0006).

## Exit criterion

On the owner suite (`testdata/user-docs`, ten documents) under `judge.policy: auto`:

1. No invented value is accepted without a judge: every job with `verification: grounded`
   has all its critical leaves in the document text, and the event-invoice pattern is never
   `accepted` with `verification: grounded`.
2. Emails skip the judge when grounded: the email cases end `accepted` with
   `verification: grounded` and no judge call, except the deterministically sampled ones.
3. PDFs are processed without caller-supplied pages: with an extractor and judge that do not
   read `application/pdf`, every PDF of the suite runs from the bytes alone (text or
   rendered pages), and no hand-rasterized copy is used.

The TravelInbox conformance suite (`test/conformance`) passes unchanged under the default
`always`.

### 02-01 Normalization, numbers and dates

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 02 Grounding check · Status: done · Depends on: v0.3

Commit: `1dc56f0`.

**Goal.** `internal/ground` can decide whether one candidate value occurs in a document's text,
robustly against case, diacritics, whitespace, punctuation, number formatting and the
common printed forms of dates and times.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- Table tests per file: diacritics and Turkish casing; punctuation at edges versus inside;
  `1234.5` found in `1.234,50 EUR` and `1,234.50`; `12` not found in `123`; dates found in
  every listed form and not found when day and month are swapped in an unambiguous date
  (`2027-03-21` against `03/21/2027` is a miss, since the US order is not in the table).
- A fuzz test (`FuzzNormalize`) shows that normalization never panics and is idempotent.
- Benchmark: normalizing a 200 kB text takes under 20 ms on one core.

### 02-02 `Check` and schema annotations

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 02 Grounding check · Status: done · Depends on: 01

Commit: `1dc56f0`.

**Goal.** `ground.Check(docText, candidate, schema, opts)` walks a candidate and returns one feedback
entry per ungrounded leaf, honouring the schema's enums and `x-vde-grounding` annotations.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- Table tests with planted invented values: each invented leaf yields exactly one entry with
  the right pointer. Correct values printed in another format (number, date, case,
  diacritics) yield none.
- The event-invoice test flags the event dates, venue, organiser and every traveller name,
  and nothing else. `CriticalMissed > 0`.
- Annotation tests: `skip` and `derived` leaves are counted as skipped. With one `critical`
  property, misses elsewhere are reported but do not count in `CriticalMissed`. An
  annotation on an array applies to its items.
- No test output or error carries a candidate value (an assertion like the redaction tests
  in `internal/logging`).

### 02-03 Document text for grounding

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 02 Grounding check · Status: done · Depends on: 02

Commit: `0bcafe9`.

**Goal.** Every representation carries the text the grounding check runs against, or says that there
is none. The text is computed once, never sent to a model unless the representation is
text, and never logged.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- `representation_test.go`: groundable text for `.txt`, `.md`, `.html` under both policies
  (visible text in both), and `.eml`. Not groundable for an image, for a PDF until 04-03, or
  for pages.
- A grounding test over `examples/samples/booking.html` and `booking.eml` with the sample
  schema and a correct candidate yields no entries.
- The redaction test passes with `GroundText` in its list.

### 03-01 `always` and `never` paths, grounding repair

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 03 Auto decision in the loop · Status: done · Depends on: 01-02, 02-03

Commit: `0f7860f`.

**Goal.** `extract.Run` takes the job's policy. Under `always` and `auto`, a schema-valid candidate
with ungrounded critical leaves goes back to the extractor once before any judge call. Under
`never`, the loop ends `extracted` without a judge.

**Acceptance criteria.**

Scenario tests in `internal/extract` with the fake provider (new scripts under
`testdata/scenarios/judge-policy/`):

- `always`, invented date on attempt 1, corrected on attempt 2: 2 extractor calls, 1 judge
  call, `accepted`, `verification: judge`, no `grounding` source in the feedback.
- `always`, invented date persists after the repair: the judge is asked on attempt 2. If it
  accepts, the job is `accepted` with `verification: judge` (judge authority, contract §5).
- `always` with `max_attempts: 1`: no repair, the judge is asked at once.
- `never`, valid candidate: 1 extractor call, 0 judge calls, `extracted`,
  `verification: none`, grounding entries present as advisory.
- `never`, schema violation on every attempt: `rejected`.
- `TestRun_scenarioSuiteUnchanged` still passes for every v0.3 script whose candidates are
  grounded (scripts with ungrounded candidates are listed in the test with their new call
  counts).

### 03-02 `auto` gate, sampling and metrics

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 03 Auto decision in the loop · Status: done · Depends on: 01

Commit: `0f7860f`.

**Goal.** Under `auto`, a candidate that passes the grounding gate is accepted without a judge, with
`verification: grounded`. A deterministic sample goes to the judge anyway, and disagreements
are counted. Everything else goes to the judge.

**Acceptance criteria.**

Scenario tests (fake provider):

- `auto`, text document, grounded valid candidate, not sampled: 1 extractor call, 0 judge
  calls, `accepted`, `verification: grounded`.
- **Exit criterion:** `auto`, candidate with an invented date, in every variant (repair
  fails, last attempt, schema retry first): the outcome is never `accepted` with
  `verification: grounded`. It is accepted only by a judge call.
- `auto`, page-image document (ungroundable): the judge is always asked.
- `auto`, schema violation then a valid grounded candidate: the judge is asked (a schema
  retry occurred).
- Sampling: with rate 1, the grounded candidate is judged. A judge reject increments the
  disagreement counter and feeds the next attempt. With rate 0, never judged.
  `TestSampled` checks that the same key always gives the same answer and that the share
  is near the rate over 10,000 random keys (±1 %).
- Metrics tests: one decision per job with the right labels.

### 04-01 `internal/pdf` on go-pdfium (WebAssembly)

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 04 PDF preprocessing · Status: done · Depends on: v0.3

Commit: `0bcafe9`.

**Goal.** A small, bounded, cgo-free package that opens a PDF, counts its pages, reads each page's
text and renders a page to PNG at a given dpi.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- Tests on `examples/samples/booking.pdf`: page count, non-empty text containing the
  sample's booking reference, and a page render whose PNG decodes to the expected size at
  150 dpi (A4 is 1240 × 1754).
- A generated multi-page PDF (written by the test from a tiny hand-made PDF template in
  `internal/pdf/testdata/`, 25 pages) is refused with `ErrTooManyPages` at `MaxPages: 20`.
  A 3-page one gives 3 texts in order.
- Corrupt bytes, an encrypted PDF and a zero-byte input give typed errors, never panics.
  `FuzzOpen` runs in CI for 30 s.
- Concurrency: 8 goroutines on `Workers: 1` all finish, serialized. A call that exceeds
  `CallTimeout` returns an error and the next call succeeds on a fresh instance.
- Benchmarks (`BenchmarkText`, `BenchmarkRender150`) are recorded in the job outcome, warm
  cache, against the ADR 0010 budget.
- `go build` with `CGO_ENABLED=0` for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64 in CI.

### 04-02 `documents.pdf` and representation selection

Version: v0.4 · Epic: 04 PDF preprocessing · Status: done · Depends on: 01

Commit: `0bcafe9`.

**Goal.** When a role's groups cannot read `application/pdf` natively, the service chooses text or
rendered pages per `documents.pdf.mode`, instead of refusing a PDF that arrives without
pages.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- `representation_test.go`: a text-rich PDF under `auto` with text-only and image-only
  groups gives `Text` and `Pages` respectively. A PDF with one blank page under `auto`
  gives `Pages`. `text` with too little text still gives `Text`. `native` without pages
  gives `unsupported_document`. Caller pages win over rendering. A PDF-capable pair still
  gets `Native`.
- Determinism: selecting twice on the same bytes gives the same `Kind`, `Source` and parts.
- `internal/api/submit_test.go`: a PDF over `max_pages` without native readers answers
  `415`. A PDF with a PDF-capable group is unaffected.
- `internal/worker/pool_test.go`: a rendered-pages job reaches the fake provider with
  `image/png` parts at the expected size. A resumed job renders identical bytes.
- Version tests: a PDF-capable configuration keeps its v0.3 hash.
